Highlights of Solar and Geomagnetic Activity
16 - 22 November 2026

Solar activity ranged between very low and moderate levels
(R1-Minor). Region 4576 (S18, L=099, class/area=Cai/070 on 17 Nov)
produced an M2.4 (R1) flare at 19/1324 UTC, the strongest of the
period. Regions 4578 (S11, L=095, class/area=Eai/070 on 18 Nov) and
4572 (N12, L=057, class/area=Hsx/060 on 10 Nov) also produced
low-level R1 events. The other nine numbered active regions were
mostly simple and quiet or decayd to plage. No significant
Earth-directed eruptions were observed in available coronagraph
imagery. 

No proton events were observed at geosynchronous orbit.

The greater than 2 MeV electron flux at geosynchronous orbit reached
high levels on 16-17 Nov but decreased to moderate levels over 18-22
Nov due to periods of sustained southward Bz in the IMF. 

Geomagnetic field activity was ranged from quiet to G1 (Minor)
geomagnetic storm levels. G1 conditions were observed on 19 Nov
following a period of sustained southward Bz during the influence of
a weak positive polarity CH HSS. The remainder of the summary period
was at quiet to unsettled levels. 

Forecast of Solar and Geomagnetic Activity
23 November - 19 December 2026

Solar activity is expected to be at very low levels, with a slight
chance for R1 (Minor) radio blackouts, throughout the outlook
period. 

No proton events are expected at geosynchronous orbit.

The greater than 2 MeV electron flux at geosynchronous orbit is
expected to reach high levels on 23-26 Nov, 29 Nov - 03 Dec, and
06-15 Dec due to the anticipated effects from multiple, recurrent,
CH HSSs. The remainder of the outlook period is likely to be at
normal to moderate levels. 

Geomagnetic field activity is likely to range from quiet to G1
(Minor) geomagnetic storm levels. G1 conditions are likely on 27 Nov
and 04 Dec; active conditions are likely on 23 Nov, 26 Nov, 28 Nov,
05 Dec, and 16 Dec; unsettled conditions are likely on 29 Nov, 03
Dec, 06 Dec, and 15 Dec. All elevated levels in geomagnetic activity
are due to the anticipated effects from multiple, recurrent, CH
HSSs. The remainder of the outlook period is likely to be at mostly
quiet levels.
