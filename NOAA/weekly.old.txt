Highlights of Solar and Geomagnetic Activity
09 - 15 November 2026

Solar activity ranged from low to moderate levels. Region 4566 (S06,
L=147, class/area=Dkc/520 on 08 Nov) produced an impulsive M1.7
(R1-Minor) flare at 11/2121 UTC, the strongest of the period. Only
C-class X-ray activity was observed on the remaining days (09-10 Nov
and 12-15 Nov). Other activity included a Type II (estimated shock
velocity of 1,972 km/s) and Type IV radio sweeps from Region 4579
(N15, L=350, class/area=Hsx/170 on 15 Nov) during a C2.8/Sf flare at
14/2127 UTC. A subsequent CME was observed in coronagraph imagery
and analysis and modeling of the event suggested the potential for
the periphery of the CME to pass by Earth late on 14 Nov to early on
15 Nov. No other CMEs observed during the reporting period were
identified as Earth-directed. 

Although 13 other numbered active regions were tracked rotating
across the visible disk this week, all were relatively small,
simple, and absent of significant activity. 

No proton events were observed at geosynchronous orbit.

The greater than 2 MeV electron flux at geosynchronous orbit reached
high levels 09-15 Nov due to influence from a negative polarity
coronal hole HSS. 

Geomagnetic field activity was reached unsettled levels on 09-11 Nov
under the waning phase of a negative polarity CH HSS. Quiet
conditions were observed over 12-15 Nov. 

Forecast of Solar and Geomagnetic Activity
16 November - 12 December 2026

Solar activity is expected to be at low levels with a chance for
M-class activity (R1-R2/Minor-Moderate) throughout the outlook
period. 

No proton events are expected at geosynchronous orbit.

The greater than 2 MeV electron flux at geosynchronous orbit is
expected reach high levels on 16 Nov, 19-20 Nov, 22-26 Nov, 29
Nov-03 Dec, and 06-12 Dec due to the anticipated influence of
multiple, recurrent, coronal hole HSSs. The remainder of the outlook
is likely to be at normal to moderate levels. 

Geomagnetic field activity is expected to range from quiet to G1
(Minor) geomagnetic storm levels. G1 conditions are likely on 17-18
Nov, 27-28 Nov, and 04 Dec. Active conditions are likely on 16 Nov,
20-21 Nov, 26 Nov, and 05 Dec. Unsettled levels are likely on 19
Nov, 22 Nov, 29 Nov, 03 Dec, and 06 Dec. All elevated levels of
geomagnetic activity are due to the anticipated influence of
multiple, recurrent, coronal hole HSSs. The remainder of the outlook
period is expected to at mostly quiet levels.
